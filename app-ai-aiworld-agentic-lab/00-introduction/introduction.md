# Introduction

## About this Workshop

Oracle AI Agent Studio is a platform for building and deploying agentic applications within Oracle Fusion Cloud Applications. These apps use coordinated AI agents to analyze business data, identify priorities, recommend next steps, and carry out actions within business workflows. By combining conversational assistance, interactive information displays, and automated tasks, agentic apps help employees move from reviewing information to making decisions and completing work. AI Agent Studio enables organizations to configure these experiences around their specific business needs

Estimated Completion Time: 60 minutes

### **Objectives**

Learn about AI Agent Studio agentic applications and the workflows that drive them. 

![Introduction Image](images/intro_image_2.png =80%x*)
By the end of this lab, participants will:
* Have a high-level understanding of how agentic applications are built.
* Experience building an agentic enabled workflow with two paths.
* Add the necessary nodes and configure the workflow for the agentic application
* Build an agentic application with your workflow and execute it.
